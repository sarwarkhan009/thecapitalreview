The Capital Review - frontend demo (thecapitalreview.in)
Open index.html in a browser. Plain HTML/CSS/JS, no framework.
Pages: index.html (Home), category.html?c=markets (category template, all categories), article.html?id=0 (article template), about.html.
NOTE: All headlines, article text, market numbers and ticker are DEMO CONTENT and must be replaced with original content before public launch. Content lives in data.js.
Images in assets/ are self-generated illustrations (no third-party photos). logo.png is the client's logo.
